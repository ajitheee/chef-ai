# Digital Chef AI: your first week

**Link:** https://chef-ai-khaki.vercel.app
**Login:** the email and password I gave you in person. Keep them private.

## Five minutes to your first sheet

1. Sign in and open **Library**. Your four Centerpointe recipes are there, plus a test library you can ignore.
2. Open a recipe and tap **Scale this recipe**. Enter today's covers and tap **Scale recipe**. A sheet takes about 40 seconds.
3. Read the sheet: scaled quantities with the reason wherever they are not linear, batching, holding, the pull list in ordering units, and the accuracy checks at the top. **Print** it, or download the pull list as **CSV**. One tap each for the HACCP summary, prep list and SOP.
4. Under a sheet, **Refine this sheet** takes a plain instruction, for example "drop to 400 covers" or "make it vegetarian".

## What the tags mean

- **Draft, not yet tested:** every generated sheet starts here. It means the yield has not been confirmed by a kitchen test yet.
- **Verify label:** a prepared product (pesto, ranch, teriyaki) whose allergens depend on the brand. Check the tub.
- **Standard yield, verify with a test batch:** the sheet used a book yield because it does not have yours yet.
- **Built-in estimate** at the top of a sheet: the AI engine was unavailable and a rough estimate was shown instead. Treat it as rough, and tell me.

## Make it yours

- **Kitchen memory** (under the recipe form): write a correction in one sentence, for example "our combi yields 48%, not 45%". It applies to every sheet from then on, and each sheet lists the notes that shaped it.
- **Yields:** enter a measured number, for example pork shoulder, cook, 62. Your numbers outrank the book from then on, and the sheet says "verified".
- When a card cooks out right, open it in Library and mark it **Tested**. Sheets scaled from a Tested card carry that tag instead of Draft. Any edit to the card puts it back to Draft, and the previous version is kept.

## What I need back after the first service

The printed sheet with your pen marks, or a photo of it. Every number you would change is the most useful thing you can give me.

## If something is wrong

Text me. Nothing you do in the app can lose a recipe: every saved change keeps the version before it, and Backup (under the recipe form) downloads everything as one file.
